The Backyard Revival

Specialty
Cali Scaping
Type
Backyard
Status
Completed

From bare, muddy ground to a backyard the family uses every day.

A complete backyard transformation by Cali Scaping: a curved lawn, a flagstone path with stepping stones, raised planter beds and new planting along the house.

The transformation

  1. Before

    Bare soil, standing water after rain and no way to use the space.

  2. Vision

    A soft curve of lawn framed by planting, with a stone path that ties the patio to every part of the yard.

  3. After

    A finished backyard with lawn, flagstone, raised beds and lighting, ready for everyday life.
